How the price is worked out

  • Material = pieces × blank cost × (1 + waste %). Laser time = pieces × laser minutes × (1 + waste %) ÷ 60 × machine $/hour. A ruined piece costs its blank and its burn again.
  • Your time = (pieces × hands-on minutes × (1 + waste %) + setup + design minutes) ÷ 60 × your $/hour. Full cost = material + laser + your time + packing.
  • The price before fees is full cost ÷ (1 − margin). On Etsy, list price = (that + $0.25 + $0.20) ÷ (1 − 6.5% − 3%), so after the fees you keep the price before fees.
  • Etsy US fees, from Etsy's help pages, read 26 November 2026: "Etsy charges 6.5% of the total order amount", "The listing fee is a flat rate charge of $0.20 USD per listing" (Etsy Fee Basics), and payment processing for the United States "3% + 0.25 USD" (Payment Processing Fees). The listing fee is counted once per order. Offsite Ads and sales tax are not counted.
  • The "skip" line prices the same order with 0% waste and no setup or design time, then shows the profit at that price against the full cost.

Questions

What does the free calculator do?

You type one order: pieces, blank cost, waste, laser and hands-on minutes, setup, design, packing, machine rate, your pay and margin. You get the price to charge, the price per piece, the Etsy fees, the profit you keep and the loss if you skip waste and setup. Use it for as many orders as you like.

Why price backwards from Etsy's fees?

Etsy takes 6.5% of the order and 3% + $0.25 for payment processing, plus $0.20 for the listing. If you add those to your cost after picking a price, the fees come out of your margin. The calculator solves the list price so that after the fees you still keep the price you wanted.

What is a fair machine rate?

Work it out from your own laser: purchase price divided by the hours you expect it to run, plus tube or module wear and power. Published guides put a 60 W CO2 laser at about $5.44 per running hour (TrueTradeCost) and sample CO2 and diode lasers under $1 an hour (Ardent Workshop), so the rate depends on your own machine.

I sell outside the US. Does it still work?

Choose "Direct" for craft fairs, your own site or cash sales, and no Etsy fees are counted. The Etsy fees on this page are the US rates.
