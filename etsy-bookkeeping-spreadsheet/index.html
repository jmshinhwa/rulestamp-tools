Why a dashboard number is not your books

Etsy pays you sales minus every fee, label and buyer payback, so your bank deposits never match the sales figure in Shop Manager. Record sales at the full amount and each Etsy fee as an expense, or the fees end up taxed as if they were profit. Your 2026 books close on December 31, 2026; the US return with Schedule C is due April 15, 2027.
