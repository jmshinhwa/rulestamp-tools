# Video compressor – compress to an MB limit, batch to ZIP, no upload

[https://jmshinhwa.github.io/rulestamp-tools/video-compressor/](https://jmshinhwa.github.io/rulestamp-tools/video-compressor/)

## What it does

Video compressor – pick the limit, see the size first, get a file under it Example (made-up sample clip on the page): 10 s, 1280×720, 3.91 MB → 0.97 MB under a 1 MB limit, predicted 0.97 MB before you press Compress. Pick Gmail 25 MB, Discord 20 MB or any MB: the predicted size shows BEFORE you compress, and a pass that comes out over the limit is re-encoded lower on its own, so the download is under the number you picked. Compressed on your own device: nothing uploaded, no ads, no watermark, no upload cap. If you cancel, batch, saved limits, history and CSV lock; the one-video compressor stays free.

## How to open

Open it in any browser: https://jmshinhwa.github.io/rulestamp-tools/video-compressor/ — nothing to install, your entries stay in your browser. Offline: download this folder and open `index.html`.
