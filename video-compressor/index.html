Why is the size shown before I compress?

The size of a video is bitrate × length. We take your limit, keep 3% for the MP4 index, give the sound its share and print the rest as the video bitrate and frame size. If the encoder still overshoots, the next pass lowers the bitrate by the overshoot – and if the encoder ignores the bitrate, the frame gets smaller – at most 4 passes.

Which limits do the buttons use?

Gmail: 25 MB per message for personal accounts (above that Gmail turns the file into a Drive link). Discord: 20 MB without Nitro, 50 MB with Nitro Basic. Each button shows the date we read the official help page.

Does a long or 4K video work?

Yes, as long as your device can decode it; the file is read in pieces, not uploaded. Long 4K files take minutes on a laptop. The result is held in memory, so very large results need a computer rather than a phone. Tested so far in desktop Chrome; phone browsers are not tested yet.
