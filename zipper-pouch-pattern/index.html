Questions

Do I have to print anything?
No. Every piece is a rectangle with square cut-outs, so the chart lets you cut with a ruler. The printed piece helps if you like a paper template, and it carries the turning-gap marks.
Is seam allowance included?
Yes, 1 cm on every panel edge. The blue dashed line on the piece is the seam line, and the chart says which layers are cut without it.
Has a pouch been sewn from it?
No sewn sample is shown. The pieces are drafted and checked by code; the guide uses line drawings, labelled as drawings.
