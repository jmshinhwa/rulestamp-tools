How the numbers are worked out

  • Monthly cost of a stack line: a monthly price as is, a yearly price ÷ 12, a 4-week price × 13 ÷ 12, a weekly price × 52 ÷ 12, a quarterly price ÷ 3. Lines marked "team" are split equally across the accounts.
  • Cost of the last 4 weeks = monthly stack × 12 ÷ 52 × the number of logged weeks that start in the 28 days up to the as-of date, plus your hours × the value of one hour for each of those weeks (split equally across accounts). Cost per meeting = that cost ÷ meetings booked.
  • Charges: each dated line is rolled forward by its billing period to its next charge on or after the as-of date.
  • Acceptance is flagged when an account's last 4 weeks fall below 70% of its own rate in the 4 weeks before. The weekly invite limit is the number you choose; the page does not claim a LinkedIn limit.
  • This page sends nothing to LinkedIn and needs no LinkedIn login. LinkedIn's help page "Prohibited software and extensions" (read 2026-11-15) says it does not permit browser extensions or bots that "automate activity on LinkedIn's website", and that members who use them "risk having their accounts restricted or shut down".
  • The numbers are the ones you type into the page.
