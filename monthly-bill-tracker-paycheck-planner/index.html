How the dates are worked out

Safe pay-by day = the due date, or the last bank business day before it when the due date is a Saturday, Sunday or a day the Federal Reserve Banks are closed. Some lenders accept a payment on the next business day; planning with the earlier day means the money is there either way.

Bank holidays come from the Federal Reserve's own list, "Holidays Observed by the Federal Reserve System 2026-2030" (federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/k8.htm, read on Nov 18, 2026). In December 2026 that is Christmas Day, Friday Dec 25; Friday Jan 1, 2027 is New Year's Day, so a payday scheduled for Jan 1 lands on Thu Dec 31 when your employer pays the business day before.

Which paycheck pays a bill = the last paycheck that lands on or before the bill's safe pay-by day. Bills before the first paycheck of the month are paid from the cash in the account on the 1st.
