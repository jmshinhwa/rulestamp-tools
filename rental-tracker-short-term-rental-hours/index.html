Questions

What does this rental tracker work out?

From 18 inputs (year, rental, tax rate, MAGI, the stays box, the hours log, nine expense amounts and three carried-in amounts) it gives 6 results: the average stay in nights, personal-use days against the 14-day / 10% limit, your hours, the hours you still need, Schedule E line 21, and the tax lost this year if the loss is suspended.

Why does a 7-day average stay matter?

IRS Publication 925 says an activity is not a rental activity when the average period of customer use is 7 days or less: total days in all rental periods divided by the number of rentals. A loss from it is then passive unless you materially participate, and the $25,000 allowance for rental real estate does not reach it.

Why do my cleaner's hours count against me?

Treas. Reg. 1.469-5T(a)(3) is met when you participate more than 100 hours and not less than any other individual, owners or not. Each person is compared on their own, so one cleaner with 102.5 hours beats your 81.5. Your spouse's hours are pooled with yours. Over 500 hours of your own meets test (a)(1) whatever others log.

What is the 14-day personal use limit?

Publication 527: you use a unit as a home when your personal days are more than the greater of 14 days or 10% of the days rented at a fair price. Family, below-fair-rent and swap nights count as personal. Then rental expenses are capped at rents in Worksheet 5-1 order and the rest carries to next year.
