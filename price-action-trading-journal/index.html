Spread or slippage per symbol ($ per 1 lot or 1 contract, round trip)

Your account's typical spread × value per point. EURUSD: 0.2 pip × $10 per pip and lot = $2.00. Futures: ticks you usually lose × tick value (MNQ 1 tick = $0.50, MES 1 tick = $1.25). An empty box counts as 0.

How it is computed

  • Spread per trade = lots × your spread per lot. In a broker export it is already inside the result; it is only added back so you see the edge before costs: gross = result + spread.
  • Net = gross − spread − extra slippage + commission + swap (commission and negative swap come with a minus from the export). Costs = gross − net. If the export has no fee column, commission = lots × your commission per lot.
  • Expectancy per trade = sum ÷ number of trades in the setup; in R = net expectancy ÷ your risk per trade.
  • Break-even win rate = average loss ÷ (average win + average loss), both net. If your win rate is below it, the setup loses money.
  • Cost buffer = net ÷ lots traded: how many dollars per lot your fills can get worse before the setup lands at 0.
  • Worked example A, "1-minute breakout": result +$45.00 + spread 14 × 1.00 lot × $2.00 = $28.00 → gross +$73.00; commission 14 × $7.00 = $98.00; $73.00 − $28.00 − $98.00 = −$53.00; −$53.00 ÷ 14 = −$3.79 per trade.
