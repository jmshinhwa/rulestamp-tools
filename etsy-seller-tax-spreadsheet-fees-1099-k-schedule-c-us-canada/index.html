Where the statement files are

  1. On Etsy.com open Shop Manager, then Finances, then Monthly statements.
  2. Pick a month and press Download CSV. Do it for each month of the year.
  3. Drop all the files on the box above. UK sellers: add both April statements; lines before 6 April and after 5 April are left out by date.

The 1099-K shows the gross: the IRS says box 1a reports the total, or gross, dollar amount of payments. Your deposits are what is left after Etsy takes its fees, ads, labels and the money it sends back to buyers. That difference is what the lines on the left put back in your books. In the made-up example: $857.00 sent back to buyers (line 2), $897.60 of Etsy Ads and Offsite Ads (line 8), $2,214.30 of Etsy fees (line 10) and $2,300.80 of shipping labels (line 18), together $6,269.70, with $1,085.40 still in the Etsy balance on 31 December.

The official pages behind each line (read 26 Nov 2026)

United States

  • IRS, Schedule C (Form 1040): line 1 gross receipts or sales, line 2 returns and allowances, line 8 advertising, line 10 commissions and fees, line 18 office expense. These are the line numbers of the 2025 form, the newest one published; check them on the 2026 form when it comes out.
  • IRS, Instructions for Schedule C, line 18: "Include on this line your expenses for office supplies and postage."
  • IRS, Understanding your Form 1099-K: an online marketplace sends one when your payments total over $20,000 in more than 200 transactions, and "you must report all income on your tax return" either way.

Canada

  • CRA, guide T4002, chapter 4: amount 3A, gross sales, commissions or fees (including GST/HST collected or collectible), and line 8000.
  • CRA, guide T4002, chapter 5: line 8521 advertising, line 8871 management and administration fees including bank charges, line 9270 other expenses, line 9275 delivery, freight and express.
  • CRA, when to register for the GST/HST: a small supplier stays at or under $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters.

United Kingdom

  • HMRC, SA103S Self-employment (short), 6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026: box 9 turnover, box 10.1 trading income allowance, box 17 interest and bank and credit card financial charges, box 18 phone, fax, stationery and other office costs, box 19 other allowable business expenses, box 20 total allowable expenses.
  • HMRC, SA103S notes: postage is an allowable expense, and with turnover below £90,000 you may put just the total in box 20.
  • GOV.UK, the trading allowance: up to £1,000 a year, and you cannot deduct other expenses if you claim it.
  • GOV.UK, Self Assessment deadlines: the online return and the payment for 2025 to 2026 by 11:59pm on 31 January 2027.
  • GOV.UK, selling on a digital platform: platforms report your details and your sales income to HMRC.

What it does, and what it does not

  • It sorts the lines of your own Etsy statements and adds them up by the line of your form. It does not file anything and it is not tax advice: check each line with your tax preparer.
  • It reads Etsy's monthly statement CSV. Sales on other sites, stock you bought, mileage and a home office go on the Other costs tab of the workbook, on the line you choose.
  • It does not prepare GST/HST or VAT returns, and it does not work out the tax you owe.
