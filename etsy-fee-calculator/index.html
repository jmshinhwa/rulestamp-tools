Questions

What does this Etsy fee calculator count that others leave out?

Every fee Etsy takes from one order in your country: the US$0.20 listing fee per item, the 6.5 % transaction fee, payment processing on the total including sales tax, the regulatory operating fee in force since 22 June 2026, Offsite Ads with the US$100 cap, 2.5 % currency conversion and VAT on fees. Example (made up): $30 + $6 shipping in the US keeps $17.57 after $14.50 of costs.

Why does a UK or EU seller keep less than a US calculator says?

Three lines US calculators skip: processing is 4 % plus a fixed fee, many countries pay a regulatory operating fee (UK 0.48 % since 22 June 2026, was 0.32 %), and Etsy adds VAT on all its fees unless a VAT ID is on file. A £100 UK sale, no shipping, keeps £86.40; without the regulatory fee and VAT the sum would say £89.15.

Why not just use a free calculator or a spreadsheet?

For one sale, the free calculator here is enough. No single-sale calculator can tell you what last month really left you: Etsy Ads, shipping labels, money sent back to buyers and fees on every line only show up in Etsy's statement. A spreadsheet can do it if you copy every line by hand each month and keep the fee rates current yourself.
